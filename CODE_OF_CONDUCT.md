# Code of conduct

metapatrol projects are places for technical work. Be the kind of
collaborator you would want on your own project.

## Expected

- Be respectful and assume good intent; disagree about code, not about people.
- Stay on topic, and give others the context they need to help you.
- Accept that maintainers decide what a project takes on, and that "no" or
  "not now" is a valid answer.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language of any kind.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing a discussion.
- Publishing other people's private information.
- Sexualised language or imagery.

## Scope

This applies in every metapatrol repository — issues, pull requests,
discussions and reviews — and wherever someone represents a project.

## Reporting

Use GitHub's *Report content* on the comment, issue or pull request in
question. Reports to the repository's maintainers are seen only by them.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions, lock conversations, and
block people from the organisation, temporarily or for good, for behaviour
that does not fit this code. They will say why when it is safe to.

This code draws on the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
